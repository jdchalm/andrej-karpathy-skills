# Evidence

Does the essay support its claims with sources tied to specific claims. Since 2026-08-20 a single source caps this criterion at 3.

Sources: raw/criteria/rubric-v1.md, raw/assessments/essay-001.md, raw/assessments/essay-002.md, raw/assessments/essay-003.md, raw/feedback/reviewer-note-2026-08.md

## Rubric text
5: multiple specific sources, each tied to a claim. 3: some sources, loosely tied. 1: assertions only.

## Examples by score
- **5**: [essay-003](../cases/essay-003.md). Six sources, each attached to a claim, counter-source addressed.
- **4**: [essay-001](../cases/essay-001.md). Two cited studies with figures; one unsourced claim.
- **4, disputed**: [essay-002](../cases/essay-002.md). One source, applied carefully. See contradiction below.

> **Contradiction.**
> raw/assessments/essay-002.md (reviewer B, 2026-06-02): "Scored 4 because it is applied carefully even
> though it is a single source."
> raw/feedback/reviewer-note-2026-08.md (both reviewers, 2026-08-20): "A single source, however well
> used, should cap at 3."
> Resolution: the calibration note is later and agreed by both reviewers. Rule going forward is in
> [single-source-evidence](../decisions/single-source-evidence.md). essay-002's score of 4 stands as
> recorded but would be 3 under the current rule.

## Edge cases
- Number of sources is necessary but not sufficient: essay-003 also tied each one to a claim.
- One unsourced claim among several sourced ones costs one point (essay-001).
