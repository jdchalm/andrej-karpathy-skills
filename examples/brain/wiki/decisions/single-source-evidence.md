# Decision: a single source caps evidence at 3

Agreed by both reviewers on 2026-08-20. Applies to assessments from that date; earlier scores stand as recorded.

Sources: raw/feedback/reviewer-note-2026-08.md, raw/assessments/essay-002.md, raw/criteria/rubric-v1.md

## Rule
An essay that relies on one source, however carefully applied, scores at most 3 on [evidence](../criteria/evidence.md).

## Why
Rubric v1's text for a 5 says "multiple specific sources". Reviewer B had read careful use of one source as
worth a 4 on [essay-002](../cases/essay-002.md). The calibration meeting chose the rubric's plain reading.

## Consequence
- essay-002 would score 3 on evidence today, overall 3.0 instead of 3.3. Not re-scored.
- Watch for: essays with two sources where one does all the work. Not covered by this rule yet.
