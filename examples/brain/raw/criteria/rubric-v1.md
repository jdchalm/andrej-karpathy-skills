# Essay assessment rubric v1

Adopted 2026-05-01. Three criteria, each scored 1-5. Overall = mean of the three.

## Clarity
How easy is the argument to follow?
- 5: every sentence does work; no re-reading needed
- 3: main line is clear but some paragraphs wander
- 1: reader cannot say what the essay argues

## Evidence
Does the essay support its claims?
- 5: multiple specific sources, each tied to a claim
- 3: some sources, loosely tied
- 1: assertions only

## Structure
Does the shape help?
- 5: clear intro, body sections that build, conclusion that adds something
- 3: identifiable sections, weak transitions
- 1: one block of text
